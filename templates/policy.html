Privacy policy

TravelMate stores account email, password hash, traveller details, trip dates, amounts in paise, and payment identifiers. Razorpay handles payment credentials; they are not stored here. Accounts can access their own bookings. Contact messages are stored for support.

{% else %}

Booking terms

All costs are estimates. A booking is confirmed only after server verification of a captured payment. Unpaid bookings may be cancelled. Paid cancellation and refunds require support review and only change after gateway processing. This build uses test mode.
